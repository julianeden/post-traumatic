# Changes

Phone widths only (820px and under), except the login username.

- **Top bar** on every page: calendar, work orders, checklists, punch clock. Alerts moves into the menu; logo, search, chat and the rest are hidden.
- **Login** remembers your username.
- **Schedule** is split into Schedule and More Options tabs.
- **More Options pages** (substitutions, trades, time off, pickups) show just that form, laid out as cards, under a Back button.
- **Your month** is a day-by-day agenda with a Today button. Each shift shows your partner or an open seat, with a "See spares" list.
- **Unit views** (BLS, ALS, Details) show one day at a time, with open-shift counts per day, All / Open only / Mine filters, Find a person and a per-person calendar.
- **Open shifts** you can take are green Request buttons. The site's own confirmation still runs.
- **Dispatch view** marks who is on right now, including last night's shifts that run past midnight, and names the positions Supervisor, Day, Eve, Night and Training.
- **Work orders and checklists** are one column with full-width controls.
- **Odometer** accepts any reading at or above the last one.
- **Punch clock** is one big button. More than 7 minutes early asks first.
- **Overtime form** (after a late punch out) shows the shift and how late the punch was, with full-width fields.
- **Dialogs** fit the phone.
- **Privacy**: nothing leaves traumasoft.com.
