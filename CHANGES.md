# Changes

Phone widths only (820px and under), except the login username.

- **Top bar** on every page: calendar, work orders, checklists, punch clock. Alerts moves into the menu; logo, search, chat and the rest are hidden.
- **Login** remembers your username.
- **Schedule** is split into Schedule and More Options tabs.
- **More Options pages** (substitutions, trades, time off, pickups) show just that form, laid out as cards, under a Back button.
- **Your month** is a day-by-day agenda with a Today button and the same Base dropdown as the unit views, which keeps only your shifts at that base; every page load starts on all bases. Each shift shows your partner or an open seat, with a "See spares" list. A partner who joins after midnight is found under the next day, and any stretch with no one beside you is marked.
- **Unit views** (BLS, ALS, Details) show one day at a time under a week strip with open-shift counts per day, a Base dropdown (Alpha, Bravo, Echo…), Find a person and a per-person calendar. A seat that runs through midnight also shows the seat listed under the next day that joins it, and the hours with no one beside it.
- **Colour key** for time off waits behind a "Show color key" button on every schedule view.
- **Open shifts** you can take are green Request buttons. The site's own confirmation still runs.
- **Dispatch view** marks who is on right now, including last night's shifts that run past midnight, and names the positions Supervisor, Day, Eve, Night and Training.
- **Work orders and checklists** are one column with full-width controls.
- **Odometer** accepts any reading at or above the last one.
- **Punch clock** is one big button. More than 7 minutes early asks first.
- **Overtime form** (after a late punch out) shows the shift and how late the punch was, with full-width fields.
- **Memo sign-off** (the page shown at login while a memo awaits your signature) is a memo card under its subject and sender, with a full-width employee-number box and button. The quick links give way to the logo there, as the site gives that page no menus.
- **Dialogs** fit the phone.
- **Privacy**: nothing leaves traumasoft.com.
